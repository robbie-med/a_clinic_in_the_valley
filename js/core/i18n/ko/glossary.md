# Korean glossary and style (한국어 용어집)

Every translator uses these terms, so the same thing has the same name everywhere. Add to the list when you
coin a term that will recur.

## Register (문체)
- **Patients and carers speaking** (complaints, history answers, "We came back…"): plain spoken 해요체, rural
  and simple. "열이 나요.", "어젯밤부터 설사를 해요.", "아니요, 그런 건 없어요." A child's parent speaks *about*
  the child: "우리 딸이…", "우리 아들이…". Never stiff textbook language in a patient's mouth.
- **The doctor's questions** (history questions): polite 해요체. "언제부터 그랬어요?", "열이 있어요? 오르락내리락해요?"
- **Advice you give** (INSTRUCTIONS labels): polite imperative 하세요체. "약은 다 나아도 끝까지 드세요."
- **Exam findings, handbook, `learn`, `refer`, notes, hindsight, cautions, ledger**: written plain style (-다체,
  or noun-ending 개조식 for short findings). "숨이 빠르다.", "피부를 꼬집으면 천천히 돌아간다." Short findings may end
  in nouns: "열감 있음." — but keep one style per table.
- **UI** (buttons, tabs, titles): short nouns or verb stems. "가방", "만들기", "다음 환자", "저장".
- Keep it plain, like *Where There Is No Doctor* itself: everyday words before Sino-Korean medical jargon, with the
  medical name in brackets when the English gives one. "물 같은 설사", "폐렴", "콧물".

## Fixed forms
- Game title: **골짜기의 진료소** ("A Clinic in the Valley").
- The book: **『의사가 없는 곳에서』** (Where There Is No Doctor). "WTND" stays WTND in refs. Page refs: "WTND pp.151–160"
  → "WTND 151–160쪽", "p.171" → "171쪽".
- Money: "sh" (shillings) → **실링**; "12 sh" → "12실링". Coins: 동전.
- "Name in the valley" (reputation) → **골짜기 평판**.
- Seasons: Cool dry season 서늘한 건기 · Hot dry season 더운 건기 · The rains 우기 · Late rains 늦은 우기.
- Day N → N일째. Time stays 24h "07:03".
- Units stay as symbols: mg, g, ml, L, kg, mg/L, °C, %, /min. "appl." (applications) → 회 바름. "×3" stays.
- People: the player = 당신 (avoid where possible); Mother (speaker) → 어머니; You (speaker) → 나; Exam → 진찰.
- Places: village 마을, hamlet 작은 마을 (or 부락), the clinic 진료소, health centre 보건소, hospital 병원,
  mission medical stores 선교회 의약품 창고, market day 장날, village shop 마을 가게, herb seller 약초 장수,
  tool peddler 도구 행상, import house 수입상, city wholesaler 도시 도매상, district health office 군 보건소.
- Latin plant names, families and genus names stay in Latin (italic is handled by the UI). Drug names: Korean
  standard transliteration (아목시실린, 파라세타몰(아세트아미노펜), 클로로퀸, 아르테메테르-루메판트린, 퀴닌, 메벤다졸,
  알벤다졸, 퍼메트린, 벤질 벤조에이트, 메트로니다졸, 코트리목사졸, 독시사이클린, 시프로플록사신, 에리스로마이신,
  클로람페니콜, 세프트리악손, 아드레날린(에피네프린), 살부타몰, 프레드니솔론, 페노바르비탈, 디아제팜, 옥시토신,
  미소프로스톨, 에르고메트린, 황산마그네슘, 암피실린, 젠타마이신, 이소니아지드, 리팜피신, 피라진아마이드,
  에탐부톨, 스트렙토마이신, 답손, 아스피린, 이부프로펜, 코데인, 아르테수네이트, 아르테미시닌, 벤질페니실린,
  벤자틴 페니실린, 페니실린 V, 디클록사실린, 아연, 철분(황산제일철), 비타민 A, 엽산, 요오드, 오메프라졸, 제산제).
- Generated names of people and hamlets are transliterated by `names.js` (never translate them by hand).
